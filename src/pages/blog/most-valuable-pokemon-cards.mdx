# The Most Valuable Pokémon Cards in 2024: A Collector’s Guide

Pokémon cards have become much more than a childhood pastime; they are now a multimillion-dollar collectible market. Some of these cards, with their rarity and unique backstories, are valued at incredible prices. Let’s explore the most valuable Pokémon cards in 2024 and learn what makes them so special.

---

## What Makes a Pokémon Card Valuable?

The value of a Pokémon card depends on several key factors:

1. **Rarity**: Limited production or unique distribution.
2. **Condition**: Gem Mint 10 cards graded by PSA or Beckett fetch the highest prices.
3. **Historical Significance**: Cards tied to tournaments or milestones hold high collectible value.
4. **Popularity**: Fan-favorite Pokémon or iconic designs can significantly boost a card’s worth.
