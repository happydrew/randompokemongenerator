## Introduction

The Pokémon Trading Card Game (TCG) has captivated fans worldwide since its inception in 1996. Over the years, numerous sets have been released, each introducing new mechanics, Pokémon, and artwork. Understanding these sets is essential for collectors and players alike.

## Collecting Pokémon Card Sets

Collecting Pokémon card sets involves acquiring cards from various expansions, each with unique features and rarities. Collectors often focus on completing sets, obtaining rare cards, or building competitive decks.

## Conclusion

The Pokémon TCG offers a rich and diverse array of sets, each contributing to the game's evolving landscape. Whether you're a seasoned collector or a new player, exploring these sets provides a deeper appreciation of the Pokémon universe.
