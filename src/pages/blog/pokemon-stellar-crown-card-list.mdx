The Pokémon Trading Card Game (TCG) continues to captivate fans with its latest expansion, **Scarlet & Violet—Stellar Crown**. Released as part of the Scarlet & Violet series, the Stellar Crown expansion brings fresh dynamics to the Pokémon TCG. Notable inclusions are the debut of **Terapagos ex** and other unique Pokémon from Area Zero, enhancing gameplay strategies and collection value.
