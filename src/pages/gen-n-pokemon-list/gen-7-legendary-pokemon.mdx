## What Are Gen 7 Legendary Pokemon?

**Gen 7 Legendary Pokemon** are the powerful creatures introduced in **Generation 7**, featured in the games **Pokemon Sun, Moon, Ultra Sun, and Ultra Moon**. These legendary Pokemon are deeply intertwined with the Alola region’s unique lore, representing celestial bodies, guardianship, and ultra-dimensional exploration.

Generation 7 brought legendary Pokemon with bold designs, fascinating backstories, and a focus on balance and harmony in Alola’s ecosystem.

## Complete List of Gen 7 Legendary Pokemon

Here’s the list of legendary Pokemon from Generation 7:
- **Solgaleo** - A Psychic/Steel-type, known as the emissary of the sun.
- **Lunala** - A Psychic/Ghost-type, representing the guardian of the moon.
- **Necrozma** - A Psychic-type that can take on multiple forms: Dusk Mane, Dawn Wings, and Ultra Necrozma.
- **Tapu Koko** - An Electric/Fairy-type guardian deity of Melemele Island.
- **Tapu Lele** - A Psychic/Fairy-type guardian deity of Akala Island.
- **Tapu Bulu** - A Grass/Fairy-type guardian deity of Ula’ula Island.
- **Tapu Fini** - A Water/Fairy-type guardian deity of Poni Island.
- **Cosmog** - A Psychic-type pre-evolution to Solgaleo and Lunala.
- **Cosmoem** - The intermediary Psychic-type evolution between Cosmog and its final forms.
- **Magearna** - A Steel/Fairy-type mythical Pokemon created by ancient science.
- **Marshadow** - A Fighting/Ghost-type mythical Pokemon, mysterious and stealthy.
- **Zeraora** - An Electric-type mythical Pokemon, known for its incredible speed.

These Pokemon play pivotal roles in the Alola region’s story and battles.
