## What Are Gen 3 Mythical Pokemon?

**Gen 3 Mythical Pokemon** are the rare and elusive creatures introduced in **Generation 3**, first featured in **Pokemon Ruby and Sapphire**. These Pokemon are integral to the mythology of the **Hoenn region** and often appear in special events, contributing to the region's legendary history.

Generation 3 introduced several notable mythical Pokemon, including **Jirachi** and **Deoxys**, both of which have unique and powerful abilities that have made them favorites among trainers.

## Complete List of Gen 3 Mythical Pokemon

Here’s the list of mythical Pokemon from Generation 3:
- **Jirachi** - A Steel/Psychic-type, Jirachi is known as the "Wish Pokemon." With the ability to grant wishes, Jirachi has captured the hearts of fans who admire its mysterious nature and rare appearance.
- **Deoxys** - A Psychic-type, Deoxys is a shape-shifting Pokemon with the ability to change its form depending on the situation. Its connection to the extraterrestrial realm adds an extra layer of intrigue to its role in the Hoenn region.

These two **Gen 3 Mythical Pokemon** are central to the lore and the unique events within **Pokemon Ruby and Sapphire**.
