Here’s the list of mythical Pokemon from Generation 4:
- **Shaymin** - A Grass-type Pokemon, Shaymin is known for its ability to transform into the Sky Form, which is key to its role in the Pokemon lore. It represents gratitude and harmony in the world of Pokemon.
- **Darkrai** - A Dark-type Pokemon, Darkrai is infamous for its ability to induce nightmares. It plays a significant role in the Sinnoh region’s legends, often appearing as an antagonist or protector.
- **Arceus** - Known as the "Original One," Arceus is a Legendary Pokemon said to have created the Pokemon world. With its powerful abilities and numerous forms, Arceus is central to the overarching mythology of the Pokemon series.

These three **Gen 4 Mythical Pokemon** have crucial roles in the Sinnoh region’s story, making them highly sought after by trainers.
