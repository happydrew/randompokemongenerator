## What Are Gen 6 Mythical Pokemon?

**Gen 6 Mythical Pokemon** are rare and powerful creatures introduced in **Generation 6** of the Pokémon franchise, first featured in **Pokémon X and Y**. These Pokemon are deeply tied to the mythology of the **Kalos region**, and they play pivotal roles in the region’s lore, often connected to significant events and legends.

In **Generation 6**, the **mythical Pokemon** featured are **Diancie**, **Xerneas**, and **Yveltal**, each with distinct abilities that shape the battles and storylines of the Kalos region.

## Complete List of Gen 6 Mythical Pokemon

Here’s the list of **Gen 6 Mythical Pokemon**:
- **Xerneas** - A Fairy-type Pokemon known as the "Life Pokemon," Xerneas has the ability **Fairy Aura**, which boosts the power of Fairy-type moves. Xerneas is central to the Kalos region’s storyline, where it is said to bring life to the world.
- **Yveltal** - A Dark/Flying-type Pokemon called the "Destruction Pokemon," Yveltal is the counterpart to Xerneas. It is associated with destruction and is said to bring death to the world. Yveltal is an imposing force with the ability **Dark Aura**, which increases the power of Dark-type moves.
- **Diancie** - A Rock/Fairy-type Pokemon, Diancie is known as the "Jewel Pokemon" and is the result of a rare mutation in Carbink. Diancie’s **Magic Bounce** ability makes it an effective defensive Pokemon, reflecting status effects back at its foes.
