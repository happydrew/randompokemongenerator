## What Are Gen 5 Legendary Pokemon?

**Gen 5 Legendary Pokemon** are the powerful creatures introduced in **Generation 5**, featured in the games **Pokemon Black, White, Black 2, and White 2**. Known for their thematic ties to balance, truth, and ideals, these Pokemon play key roles in Unova’s mythology.

Generation 5 expanded the legendary roster with diverse designs, unique typings, and compelling backstories.

## Complete List of Gen 5 Legendary Pokemon

Here’s the list of legendary Pokemon from Generation 5:
- **Reshiram** - A Dragon/Fire-type symbolizing truth.
- **Zekrom** - A Dragon/Electric-type representing ideals.
- **Kyurem** - A Dragon/Ice-type embodying absence and potential, with Black Kyurem and White Kyurem forms.
- **Cobalion** - A Steel/Fighting-type leader of the Swords of Justice.
- **Terrakion** - A Rock/Fighting-type powerhouse.
- **Virizion** - A Grass/Fighting-type symbol of nature.
- **Keldeo** - The Water/Fighting-type mythical member of the Swords of Justice.
- **Tornadus** - A Flying-type Weather Trio member with Incarnate and Therian forms.
- **Thundurus** - An Electric/Flying-type Weather Trio member with Incarnate and Therian forms.
- **Landorus** - A Ground/Flying-type Weather Trio member known for stabilizing Unova’s ecosystems.
- **Meloetta** - The Normal/Psychic-type mythical Pokemon with an alternate Normal/Fighting Pirouette form.
- **Genesect** - A Bug/Steel-type mythical Pokemon with ancient origins and technological enhancements.

These legendary Pokemon are central to Unova’s narrative and competitive battles.
