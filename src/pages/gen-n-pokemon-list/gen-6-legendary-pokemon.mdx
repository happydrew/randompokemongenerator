## What Are Gen 6 Legendary Pokemon?

**Gen 6 Legendary Pokemon** are the powerful creatures introduced in **Generation 6**, featured in the games **Pokemon X, Y, Omega Ruby, and Alpha Sapphire**. These Pokemon play a pivotal role in the Kalos and Hoenn regions, representing themes like life, destruction, and balance.

Generation 6 expanded the legendary lineup with stunning designs, groundbreaking abilities, and memorable lore.

## Complete List of Gen 6 Legendary Pokemon

Here’s the list of legendary Pokemon from Generation 6:
- **Xerneas** - A Fairy-type symbol of life with the ability Fairy Aura.
- **Yveltal** - A Dark/Flying-type embodiment of destruction with the ability Dark Aura.
- **Zygarde** - A Dragon/Ground-type guardian of balance with multiple forms: 10%, 50%, and Complete.
- **Diancie** - A Rock/Fairy-type mythical Pokemon capable of Mega Evolution.
- **Hoopa** - A Psychic/Ghost-type mythical Pokemon with an alternate Unbound Psychic/Dark form.
- **Volcanion** - A Fire/Water-type mythical Pokemon, unique in its dual-type combination.

These legendary Pokemon are essential to the Kalos region’s narrative and competitive gameplay.
