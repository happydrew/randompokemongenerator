## What Are Gen 5 Mythical Pokemon?

**Gen 5 Mythical Pokemon** are the rare and powerful creatures introduced in **Generation 5**, first featured in **Pokemon Black and White**. These Pokemon hold a special place in the **Unova region’s** mythology, with unique abilities and captivating stories.

Generation 5 introduced **Victini**, **Keldeo**, **Meloetta**, and **Genesect** as the mythical Pokemon, each possessing distinct characteristics and roles within the lore of the Unova region.

---

## Complete List of Gen 5 Mythical Pokemon

Here’s the list of **Gen 5 Mythical Pokemon**:
- **Victini** - A Fire/Psychic-type Pokemon, Victini is known for its Victory Star ability, which boosts the accuracy of its allies’ moves. It is said to bring victory to its trainers, making it one of the most coveted mythical Pokemon.
- **Keldeo** - A Water/Fighting-type Pokemon, Keldeo is a member of the **Swords of Justice** alongside **Cobalion**, **Terrakion**, and **Virizion**. It represents justice and bravery, playing a key role in the Pokemon Black and White story.
- **Meloetta** - A Psychic/Normal-type Pokemon, Meloetta has two forms: **Aria Form** and **Pirouette Form**. Its ability to switch between these forms is a key feature that makes Meloetta a unique mythical Pokemon.
- **Genesect** - A Bug/Steel-type Pokemon, Genesect was modified by Team Plasma and is known for its powerful **Techno Blast** move. It is a genetically altered Pokemon, making it one of the most intriguing and powerful mythical Pokemon of Generation 5.
