## What Are Gen 8 Legendary Pokemon?

**Gen 8 Legendary Pokemon** are the extraordinary creatures introduced in **Generation 8**, featured in the games **Pokemon Sword and Shield**, along with their DLC expansions, **The Isle of Armor** and **The Crown Tundra**. These legendary Pokemon are deeply connected to the lore of the Galar region and its wild, untamed landscapes.

Generation 8 expands the legendary lineup with dynamic new designs, unique abilities, and thematic connections to the region’s culture.

## Complete List of Gen 8 Legendary Pokemon

Here’s the list of legendary Pokemon from Generation 8:
- **Zacian** - A Fairy/Steel-type, representing a noble hero and protector.
- **Zamazenta** - A Fighting/Steel-type, embodying a shield of valor.
- **Eternatus** - A Poison/Dragon-type, tied to the mysterious Dynamax phenomenon.
- **Kubfu** - A Fighting-type, a crucial part of martial arts traditions in the Galar region.
- **Urshifu** - A Fighting-type with two forms: Single Strike (Dark) and Rapid Strike (Water).
- **Regieleki** - An Electric-type, the fastest Pokemon in existence.
- **Regidrago** - A Dragon-type with destructive power derived from dragon energy.
- **Glastrier** - An Ice-type, symbolizing resilience and cold endurance.
- **Spectrier** - A Ghost-type, representing spectral speed and mystery.
- **Calyrex** - A Psychic/Grass-type, revered as the king of bountiful harvests.
- **Calyrex’s Forms** - Ice Rider (Calyrex + Glastrier) and Shadow Rider (Calyrex + Spectrier).

These Pokemon are central to the Galar region's mythology and gameplay.
