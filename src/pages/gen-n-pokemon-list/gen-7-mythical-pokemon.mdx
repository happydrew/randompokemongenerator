## Complete List of Gen 7 Mythical Pokemon

Here’s the list of **Gen 7 Mythical Pokemon**:
- **Marshadow** - A Fighting/Ghost-type Pokemon known as the "Shadow Pokémon." Marshadow has the unique ability **Technician** and can use its signature move **Soul-Stealing 7-Star Strike**, making it a formidable force in battle.
- **Zeraora** - An Electric-type Pokemon with the ability **Volt Absorb**. Zeraora’s high speed and offensive capabilities make it a popular choice for electric-type teams, and it is known for its move **Plasma Fists**.
- **Meltan** - A Steel-type Mythical Pokemon, Meltan has the ability **Steelworker**, which powers up Steel-type moves. Meltan plays an important role in the **Pokémon Go** and **Pokémon Let’s Go** games, where it is involved in the process of **evolution into Melmetal**.
